Your Event.
Our BBQ.

The 1000 Oaks Meat Locker brings the pit to you. We offer on-site catering for events of 60 or more guests — set up at your location with full buffet service, fresh-grilled BBQ, and everything you need to feed a crowd.

Every package includes baked beans, chili beans, garlic bread, 2 salads of your choice, 2 beverages, all paper goods & utensils, buffet tables, and chaffers with sterno. We handle the setup. You enjoy the event.
